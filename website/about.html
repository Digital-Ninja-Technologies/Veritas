About us

Getting paid should never feel like a gamble.

Veritas is escrow and borderless payments for freelancers and the people who hire them. Here’s who we are, why we built it, and what we stand for.

Veritas

noun · Latin

1. Truth.

2. A shared, visible truth about where the money is.

our idea

Our mission

To make it easier and safer for freelancers and clients to work and pay across borders. Money locked at the start, visible to both sides, and released only on approval.

Why we built Veritas

The work travels easily. The trust doesn’t.

Work has gone global faster than payments have.

A designer in Lagos can build a brand for a founder in London. A developer in Accra can ship a product for a team in Berlin. The work crosses borders in seconds, but the trust that should travel with it, and the payment that should follow it, often get left behind.

01

Freelancers start on a hope

They begin a project hoping the money will follow, then wait on invoices, chase clients and work out how to turn a foreign payment into local currency.

02

Clients pay on a hope

They hand over a budget hoping the work will arrive, often to someone they have never met, in a country they have never visited.

03

And there’s nothing in the middle

When something goes wrong, there is no neutral place the money was waiting. Both sides lose sleep.

So we put something in the middle. With Veritas, the client funds the project up front and the money is locked in escrow. It is released milestone by milestone, as the client approves the work, and lands with the freelancer in their own currency.

One shared, visible truth about where the money is, so people can get on with the work.

What we stand for

Five things we hold ourselves to.

  1. 001

    Truth first

    We are clear about where your money is, what happens next and what it costs. No surprises, no small print games.

  2. 002

    Both sides win

    Good escrow protects the client and the freelancer. If it only helps one of them, it isn’t good escrow.

  3. 003

    Money moves on approval

    Nothing is released until the people who agreed the work say it’s done. That is the whole rule, and we keep it.

  4. 004

    Built for real borders

    Local currencies, local realities. We design for how people actually get paid, not for how it looks on a slide.

  5. 005

    Own it when we’re wrong

    We fix things in the open and tell you what changed, because trust is earned in the moments that go badly.
