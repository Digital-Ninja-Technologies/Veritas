Policy

How Veritas collects, uses and protects your information when you use the app and website.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Veritas is operated by Useveritas Limited, a company incorporated as Useveritas Limited (“Veritas”, “we”, “us”). We provide an app that lets clients fund projects into escrow and lets freelancers get paid in their local currency. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the Veritas app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We collect what we need to run the service safely, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), and your role (freelancer or client). If you sign in with Apple, we receive the details Apple shares with us.
  • Contract details: project titles, descriptions, milestones, amounts, due dates, and the email address of the person you invite to a contract.
  • Payment and payout details: the payment details and references used to fund contracts in the currency you choose (for example a stablecoin transfer reference or a bank payment reference), and the bank, mobile money or other account details you provide so we can pay you out in your local currency.
  • Identity verification: where required by law or by our payment partners, documents and information used to verify who you are and to meet anti-fraud and anti-money-laundering obligations.
  • Communications: messages you send us through the contact form, email or in-app support.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, language, and how you use the app and website, including pages and screens visited.
  • Transaction records: funding, escrow, approval, release and payout events linked to your account.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and the contracts you take part in;
  • To hold funds in escrow, release milestones you approve, and pay out in your chosen currency;
  • To verify identity, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal and regulatory requirements;
  • To send service messages such as invitations, receipts, security alerts and changes to our terms;
  • To provide support, and to understand and improve the app and website;
  • To send product updates where you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • The other party to your contract. A client and a freelancer on the same contract can see the details needed to work together, such as names, contract terms, funding status and milestone status. When you invite someone by email, they will see who invited them and the contract details.
  • Service providers that help us operate Veritas, such as payment and payout partners, identity verification providers, hosting, email delivery and analytics. They may use your information only to provide their service to us and are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Authorities and advisers where we are required by law, or need to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • A successor if Veritas is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. We will tell you if your information becomes subject to a different policy.

4. International transfers

Veritas supports payments across borders, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including by our service providers. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we take steps to make sure it remains protected in line with this policy and applicable law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. After you close your account, we keep certain records, such as transaction and verification records, for as long as the law or our payment partners require, and then delete or anonymise them.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict some processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of your data. To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@useveritasapp.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Some information we are legally required to keep, and we will tell you if that applies to your request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, and limit access to personal information to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you think your account has been compromised.

8. Children

Veritas is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Veritas evolves. We will change the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email.

10. Contact

This policy is issued by Useveritas Limited. Questions about privacy? Email privacy@useveritasapp.com or use our contact page.
